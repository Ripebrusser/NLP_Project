Introduction
Cybersecurity threats increasingly surface in social media, messaging, and everyday online text not just network logs.
NLP can automatically flag suspicious or threat-related text, but most existing systems are built for English or other high-resource languages.
Malayalam users frequently mix Malayalam script, Romanized Malayalam (Manglish), and Malayalam-English code-mixed text creating transliteration noise, spelling variation, and code-switching that standard NLP pipelines aren't tuned for.
Existing Malayalam/Manglish NLP research (
DravidianCodeMix
,
DravidianLangTech
) is concentrated on offensive language, hate speech, and sentiment not cybersecurity-specific threats leaving this intersection largely unexplored.

Problem Definition
"To develop an NLP-based framework for detecting cybersecurity-related threats in Malayalam and Malayalam-English code-mixed text, addressing the challenges of low-resource language processing, Romanized Malayalam, code-switching, spelling variation, and the absence of threat-specific annotated datasets for this language pair."
=== slide8.xml ===
8
Objectives
To develop a threat-specific Malayalam and Malayalam-English code-mixed dataset for cybersecurity threat detection, built on existing Dravidian code-mixed corpora with manual threat annotation.
To preprocess and normalize Malayalam, Manglish, and code-mixed text to handle transliteration, spelling variation, and language switching.
